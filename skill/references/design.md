# Content and visual guidance

## Narrative

- Give each slide one communication job.
- Prefer a clear claim as the slide title instead of a generic topic label.
- Keep title slides minimal; put methodology and citations in notes.
- Vary adjacent silhouettes while preserving theme, grid, and typography.

## Typography

- Deck title: at least 50 pt.
- Slide title: at least 35 pt.
- Subheading/callout: at least 24 pt.
- Body: at least 16 pt.
- Shorten text or change layout before reducing type size.

## Layout

- Use equal outer margins by default.
- Avoid dashboard-style collections of tiny cards unless the content is genuinely a dashboard.
- Use images that match the intended crop and never stretch them.
- Treat overlap warnings as defects unless the overlap is explicitly declared decorative.

## Editable export

Choose native elements when the user may edit them in PowerPoint. Use `html` for web-only interactions or CSS effects, and provide a deliberate raster fallback with matching dimensions. Do not silently turn an entire deck into page screenshots.
